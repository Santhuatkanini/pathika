Privacy Policy

  • Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Pathika ("we", "us") runs guided treks and backpacking trips out of Bengaluru. This page explains what information we collect through pathikabackpacking.in, why we collect it, and how you can control it. If anything here is unclear, email us at pathikabackpacking@gmail.com.

Information you give us

Description:

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number. If you sign in with Google instead, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us — Google does not share your phone number, so we will not have it unless you add it yourself on your profile page.

If you enquire about a trek over WhatsApp or our contact form, we keep whatever you send us (name, phone number, the trek you asked about) so we can reply and, if you go ahead, arrange your booking.

Information collected automatically

Description:

Treks you mark as a favourite are saved to your account so they are there next time you sign in.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this site, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

How we use this information

Description:
  • To create and secure your account
  • To show you your saved favourites and booking history
  • To reply to enquiries and coordinate trek bookings and logistics
  • To send you a password-reset email if you ask for one

Who we share it with

Description:

We use Supabase to store accounts and bookings, and, if you choose that option, Google to sign you in. Both only receive what is needed to provide those services and do not use your data for their own advertising. We do not share your information with any other third party, and we never sell it.

Our website itself is hosted by Hostinger, who can see ordinary web server logs (like any host) but do not have access to your account data, which lives in Supabase.

How long we keep it

Description:

We keep your account and booking history for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and favourites; booking records tied to past treks may be kept for a reasonable period for accounting purposes.

Your choices

Description:
  • View or update your profile from "My Profile" once signed in
  • Email pathikabackpacking@gmail.com to request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account
  • Unlink Google sign-in at any time from your Google Account settings

Changes to this policy

Description:

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
